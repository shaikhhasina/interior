About Us

Interiors Made for the Way You Live

Swami Samarth Interior Design & Decor is based in Narhe, Pune. We design and build complete home and office interiors that balance function, comfort, and style.

Every project starts with listening. We learn how you live, work, and cook, then shape layouts, materials, and finishes that suit your taste and budget.

  • Free site visit and measurements
  • 3D design shown before work begins
  • Quality materials and careful installation
  • One team from design to handover
